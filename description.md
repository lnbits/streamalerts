Integrate Bitcoin Lightning donations into your livestream alerts via Streamlabs.

Its functions include:

- Accepting Lightning and on-chain Bitcoin donations during livestreams
- Integration with Streamlabs for Twitch, YouTube, and Facebook streams
- Donations appear directly in your Streamlabs alerts overlay
- Shareable donation page for viewers
- Works with SatsPay and Onchain Wallet extensions for enhanced functionality

Perfect for content creators and streamers who want to accept Bitcoin donations with seamless integration into their existing streaming setup.
